# CHRONOS V8.18 — Dual Clock

V8.18 separates two problems that should not share authority.

## Slow clock — economic alpha

The slow clock owns direction. A V7 candidate must pass the frozen evidence gate:
- >= 30 holdout trades
- >= 52% hit rate
- >= +0.03% mean net return
- profit factor >= 1.25
- >= 3 profitable folds

The fast clock can never manufacture a LONG or SHORT without this slow evidence.

## Fast clock — execution intelligence

The 1-second CHRONOS model is judged separately by head:
- return head vs zero-return baseline
- order-flow head vs persistence baseline
- liquidity-change head vs zero-change baseline

A fast head must both beat its naive baseline and retain calibrated intervals.

When order-flow and liquidity heads are trusted, they may time or veto a validated
slow alpha. Failure of the price head does not erase useful microstructure evidence.

All verdicts remain research/shadow only with zero paper/live authority.
